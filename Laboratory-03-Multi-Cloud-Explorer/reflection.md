# Mission Reflection

During this mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**. I found its services for Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Kubernetes interesting because they can be useful for modern applications. However, I also learned that AWS and Azure have many useful services, so choosing a cloud platform depends on what a company actually needs.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide services for computing, storage, databases, networking, security, and virtual machines. They also allow users to access resources through the cloud instead of depending only on physical servers. Although their services have different names and features, they can provide similar solutions for many business requirements.

Business requirements are important when selecting a cloud platform because every organization has different needs. For example, a company using Microsoft products may find Azure more suitable, while a company focused on AI and Machine Learning may consider GCP. Factors such as budget, performance, scalability, security, and existing technologies should be considered before making a decision.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud provider is not simply about selecting the most popular platform. I learned that I need to look at the client's situation first and then find the services that can solve their problems. The different client scenarios in this mission helped me practice making technical decisions based on business needs.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved after completing this mission. I added more research, comparison tables, recommendations, and evidence from my cloud investigations. Organizing these files in GitHub helped me see my progress and made my work look more organized and professional. Overall, this mission gave me a better understanding of AWS, Azure, and GCP and helped me think more like a cloud solutions consultant.
